# Results and Discussion

LP-Nav was trained in NVIDIA Isaac Sim on a desktop equipped with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 GPU. Each curriculum stage required approximately 12 hours of training. We used `1,024` parallel environments, with each environment containing one UAV and independently randomized start-goal configurations and obstacle motions. The UAV translational velocity was limited to `2.0 m/s`. Each policy update collected `32` transitions per environment, resulting in a rollout batch of `32,768` transitions.

The policy was optimized using PPO with a clipping ratio of `0.1`, a discount factor of `0.99`, and a GAE parameter of `0.95`. Each rollout batch was reused for four optimization epochs and divided into `16` minibatches. Adam was used for the feature encoder, actor, and critic. Their learning rates were initialized to `5e-4` in the first curriculum stage; for subsequent adaptation stages, the feature-encoder and actor learning rates were reduced to `1e-5`, while the critic learning rate was set to `1e-4`. Training followed the stage-wise curriculum described in Sec. III-C: the number of dynamic obstacles was initialized at `80` and increased by `20` between stages, while a selected checkpoint from the preceding stage initialized the next stage. Policy performance was evaluated every `1,000` training updates using the mean action of the Beta policy to remove sampling variability.
