LP-Nav builds on NavRL’s PPO-based navigation policy and retains its deployment-time VO shield. LP-Nav augments this baseline with two complementary training-time signals: a finite-horizon, TTC-aware 3D VO reward for dynamic motion conflicts and action-conditioned auxiliary losses that predict semantically separated outcomes over fixed rollout horizons to regularize the encoder shared by the actor and critic. The auxiliary predictor is not evaluated at deployment, while NavRL’s VO shield is retained unchanged.
